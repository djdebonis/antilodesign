COMPLETED PLANNING EXAMPLE — HYPOTHETICAL, NOT A CLIENT RESULT
Question: Can this contractor extend my existing floor into the next room?
Page decision: Improve the installation page; no new city page required.
Content: Installer-reviewed explanation of assessment and matching limitations.
Evidence: Approved project photographs with descriptive captions.
Next action: Inquiry with town, project description, preferred contact method.
Done: Explanation approved, permission documented, mobile inquiry received.
Review: Note inquiry fit and recurring unanswered questions alongside search data.
No promise: Neither an invisible flooring match nor a search ranking is guaranteed.
