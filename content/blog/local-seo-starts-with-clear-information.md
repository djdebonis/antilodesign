Give nearby customers a consistent picture of what you do and where you work.

## Make the basics easy to find

Your business name, services, contact options, and actual service area should be clear on the website. Add visiting information only where it reflects how the business really operates.

## Review the places people encounter you

Compare your website with your business profiles and directory listings. Identify outdated details and prioritize the places most relevant to your customers.

## Write useful local content

Explain the services you actually provide in the areas you actually serve. A page should answer a customer question, not simply repeat a city name in several headings.

## Build an honest feedback habit

Ask customers for genuine feedback and respond thoughtfully. Local visibility is an ongoing part of the business presence, not a promise of a particular map position.
