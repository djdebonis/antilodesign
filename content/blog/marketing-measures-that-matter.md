Start with the action you want a customer to take, then choose the signals worth reviewing.

## Work backward from the goal

A campaign might aim to introduce a service, encourage an inquiry, or bring people to an event. Define that purpose before choosing the numbers for a report.

## Separate activity from outcomes

Visits, impressions, and clicks can help explain what happened. They do not, by themselves, tell you whether an inquiry was relevant or a customer found what they needed.

## Connect online and offline touchpoints

A print piece can use a distinct destination URL, and an inquiry form can ask how someone found the business. These signals have limits, but they can add context to the conversation.

## Decide what you will do with the information

A good report helps answer a question: keep the message, improve the page, try another channel, or gather more evidence. Avoid building a dashboard whose numbers never influence the work.
