A few clear design decisions can connect a website, brochure, and everyday business materials.

## Start with the message

Visual consistency is easier when the words agree. Describe the business in a clear sentence, then use that direction across the website, proposals, and printed materials.

## Define a small set of visual rules

Choose the logo versions, colors, and typography that belong in everyday use. Show how they work in common situations instead of leaving every new document to interpretation.

## Adapt to the medium

A brochure is held in the hand; a sign may be read from across a room. The same identity needs different spacing, scale, and detail in different formats. Consistency leaves room for those differences.

## Make the assets easy to find

Keep approved files and a short usage guide together. Include clear filenames and examples of the most common applications so the next piece starts from the right source.
