Design the printed piece and its destination as one conversation.

## Choose one main action

A brochure, postcard, or event handout can introduce a lot of information, but the next step should be obvious. Decide whether you want someone to learn more, make an inquiry, or visit a location.

## Match the destination

Send people to a page that continues the same offer and visual direction. A general homepage may require them to search again for what interested them in the first place.

## Offer a readable alternative

If the piece uses a QR code, include a clear URL or another contact option where space allows. Check that the destination works on a phone and that the code remains readable in the actual printed format.

## Review before production

Proofread the offer, contact details, dates, and links. Confirm the printer specifications and check the final proof. A small correction before printing is easier than replacing a full run.
