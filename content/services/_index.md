If the next step is unclear, we will begin with a [marketing review](/services/marketing-consulting/). Here, we examine a specific question, such as why the website no longer explains the business well, or which marketing work deserves a limited budget. We return with findings and priorities you can use, whether we implement them together or you take them forward yourself.

If the work is already clear, we can begin with the project itself. Our services connect research and messaging with websites, email, search content, and print. We agree on what we will make, what we need from you, and how we will review it together.

We also offer [business coaching](/services/business-coaching/) when the question is about your direction as an owner: what you want to build, which commitments fit, or how to choose between competing ideas. We keep that work distinct from a marketing review, where we investigate the materials and make recommendations.
