## Make room for the decision behind the work

We might work through whether to pursue a new idea, which customers you want to serve, or how to choose between opportunities when your time is already spoken for. We connect those choices to what matters to you and what running the business will require.

We ask questions, examine assumptions, and help make the tradeoffs explicit. We bring our experience in marketing and business operations into the conversation, while keeping your goals and judgment at the center of the decision.

## A clear beginning and a useful record

We agree on the question and session length in advance. We ask you to bring the relevant context: the options you are considering, what you have tried, and any constraints. We close with the actions you have chosen and provide a written summary you can return to.

We confirm the fee before booking. A further session is a separate decision; we agree on its purpose and timing together.

## When a marketing review is a better fit

If you need us to examine your website, research an audience, or develop marketing recommendations, we scope that as [marketing consulting](/services/marketing-consulting/). Coaching provides a structured conversation around your decisions. A marketing review includes our investigation of the agreed materials and written recommendations.
