Writing has been a thread through much of my life. I’m interested in how we make sense of things, how we explain what matters to us, and how the right words can help someone see something more clearly. That interest has followed me into teaching, marketing, research, and the businesses I’ve helped build.

My background includes English writing and linguistics, graduate study in marketing, and hands-on work in ecommerce and business operations. I also work in marketing at CU Anschutz’s Department of Biomedical Informatics, where I help communicate research and research software. Each has given me a different way of looking at a problem, and a reason to keep asking questions.

With Antilo, I bring those experiences into the same conversation. I like being able to work through the idea and then make it real: the copy, the design, the website, and the details that connect them. I care about making something useful and making sure it still sounds like the person behind it.

I’m based in the Denver area, and the mountains are a meaningful part of my life. Health, movement, and the outdoors are close to my heart. I bring that same curiosity to people building something they care about, whatever field they’re in.
