### David J DeBonis

Story telling has been a thread through much of my life.

When I was 16, I decided I wanted to be a writer when I grew up. 

When I was 17, I joined the school newspaper. 

When I was 18, I moved into a van and drove across the country in search of an idea for a book.

**The search for the story** has quite literally taken me across the continent, and it continues to drive me as a writer, marketer, linguistic, and leader.

I’ve always been captivated by langauge and communication—I'm interested in how we make sense of things, how we explain what matters to us, and how **choosing the right words can help someone see something more clearly**. This curiousity has followed me through my hobbies, vocations, and businesses.

### Education and Experience

Today, I am passionate about helping people **share their story** as a means to moving forward in their businesse, project, or organization. **Stories connect us** with the work we want to do, the things we want to build, the problems we want to solve, and the people we want to serve. Let me share the journey of how I got here.

After I returned home from my venture in my van across the country, I went to the [University of Colorado Denver](https://cudenver.edu) to pursue my Bachelor's degree. As an undergraduate student, I studied English Writing and Linguistics, and I took part time jobs tutoring at the CU Denver Writing Center and researching Spanish phonetics. During this time, I spent a lot of time thinking about how our choice of words affects the way people understand a message. Later in my undergrad, I also began gaining hands-on experience in ecommerce and business operations. **This sparked my intrigue in marketing and communications as a career path.**

After graduating, I returned to school for a Master of Science in Marketing at the CU Denver Business School. During my graduate studies, I worked with a variety of people who were either starting or growing buisnesses. Here I began to cultivate a passion for the entrepreneurial spirit, and I began to spend more time and energy helping entrepreneurs and businesses owners launch, grow, and scale their businesses. What started with a small passion project and side hustles gradually morphed into the business that exists today. **This is where Antilo Consulting was born.**

{{< personal-photo "david_debonis_masters_degree_graduation.jpeg" >}}

By an unlikely chance, I was looking for jobs in 2023 when I stumbled across a teaching job at the [Venture Academy of Leadership and Entrepreneurship](https://vale.dcsdk12.org). Although it was not in my original plan, I applied, and—to everyone's surprise—I got the job. I began teaching English to entrepreneurial students and gained my teaching license through an alternative teaching program. Working at this schoool solidified my passion for entrepreneurship as a framework, practice, and way of life.

{{< personal-photo "david_debonis_teaching_outside.jpg" >}}

Towards the end of my Master's, I transitionined into marketing at The CU Anschutz Department of Biomedical Informatics. I still work there today, helping communicate research and research software and promote the department. Those experiences have given me different ways of looking at a problem, and a reason to keep asking questions.

With Antilo, I bring these experiences into the same conversation. I like being able to work through the idea and then make it real: the copy, the design, the website, and the details that connect them. I care about making something useful and making sure it still sounds like the person behind it.

I’m based in the Denver area, and the mountains are a meaningful part of my life. Health, movement, and the outdoors are close to my heart. I bring that same curiosity to people building something they care about, whatever field they’re in.
