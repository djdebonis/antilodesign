## Getting to the heart of your business

David DeBonis is the Founder and Lead Consultant at Antilo. He brings strategy, writing, design, and website development together around a question: how can a business help people understand the value it already creates?

His work moves between the story and the details that make it real. That might mean finding the words for a changing brand, designing product labels, rebuilding an online store, or turning local research into something a customer can use. He approaches those pieces as parts of the same experience, with each helping people understand the business and decide what to do next.

## Thoughtful work, from the big picture to the particulars

For [Colorado Sauce Company](/work/colorado-sauce-company/), David’s work connected a brand transition with redesigned labels, a move from Wix to Shopify, and content supporting the way customers discover and buy the products. For [TGB Flooring](/work/tgb-flooring/), he developed company and project storytelling alongside an original research resource built around 117 local home listings.

Those projects reflect the range of Antilo’s work: understanding what matters to a business, then bringing the right mix of communication, design, and technical work to the task. They also reflect an attention to substance, whether that means preserving a company’s roots through a name change or being precise about what a research finding can tell a homeowner.

## A direct connection to the work

Today, Antilo is led and delivered by David. When you bring a project to Antilo, you work directly with the person helping shape the direction and carry it through.

Small businesses and startups are central to that focus, alongside established companies ready for a new chapter. Health and wellness are a particular area of interest, with the same thoughtful approach extending to businesses in other fields. The starting point is your business: what you care about, what your customers need, and where you want to go.
