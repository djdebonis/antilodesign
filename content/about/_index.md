There’s a lot that goes into a small business that never makes it onto the website. The years spent learning a craft. The reason someone started. The little things customers appreciate but might never think to mention. Those details matter. They help people understand who you are and why they might want to work with you.

We bring writing, design, research, and website development together around that understanding. The work might begin with a clearer message, a new online store, or a plan for reaching people nearby. We look at how those pieces fit into the business, then carry the work through to something you can use.

## Start with the business in front of us

A good idea can still need direction. An established business can still need a better way to explain itself. We work with people at both stages, paying attention to their goals, their resources, and the kind of business they want to build. There should be a reason behind what we recommend.

We’re especially interested in health, wellness, and the outdoors. We also enjoy working with people whose care shows up in very different ways: the products they make, the craft they’ve practiced for years, or the communities they bring together. What matters is understanding what makes the business worth building, and helping that come through.

## Work directly with David

Antilo is led by David DeBonis. You work directly with the person asking the questions, shaping the message, and building the work. That keeps the conversation close to the project and gives the details somewhere to land.
