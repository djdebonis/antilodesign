We worked through the page structure, service language, and website copy, with attention to how someone would move from a first impression to an understanding of the work. The direction called for something thoughtful and human, with enough substance for school leaders to see where a conversation might begin.

The first website draft became a place to bring those decisions together. Further work includes refining the content, gathering stronger photography, and completing the remaining pages and forms. The project is still developing, and the case study will grow with it.
